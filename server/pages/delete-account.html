Delete your Study Tracker account

Applies to the Study Tracker website and iPhone/Android app · Last updated {{UPDATED}}

Delete it yourself (fastest)

  1. Open Study Tracker and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm (email accounts are asked for their password).

This immediately and permanently deletes:

  • your sign-in account (email, user ID, and name if you had one), and
  • all your synced data: lists, tasks, calendar items, time blocks, settings and study time.

Apple sign-in access is also revoked. Data that was only on your device (for example the AI chat history) is removed when you uninstall the app or clear the browser's site data.

Can’t open the app?

Send a deletion request from the email address of your account. We will delete the account and its data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What we keep

Nothing from your account is kept after deletion. Our server's short-lived technical logs and the daily AI message counter (a number per user ID, reset every day) expire on their own.
