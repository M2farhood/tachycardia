Privacy Policy

Study Tracker (website and iPhone/Android app) · Last updated {{UPDATED}}

Study Tracker is a to-do and study planner. This page explains, in plain words, what data it uses and why. In short: no ads, no tracking, no analytics, and we never sell your data.

Without an account

You can use the whole app without signing in. Your lists, tasks, calendar, time blocks, settings and study time are then stored only on your device (in the browser's or app's local storage). We never receive them.

With an account (optional)

If you sign in (with Apple, Google or email and password), we use Google Firebase to keep your data in sync between your devices:

  • Account details: your email address, a user ID, and — if your Apple or Google account provides it — your name. Firebase Authentication stores these; passwords are handled by Firebase and are never seen or stored by us.
  • Your content: your lists, tasks, calendar items, time blocks, settings and study time are stored in Google Cloud Firestore under your account. Only your signed-in account can read or change them.

Tachycardia, the AI assistant (optional, off until you agree)

Tachycardia only works after you tap “I agree” in the app, and you can switch it off at any time in Settings → Advanced. When you use it:

  • What you type, plus the names of your lists, tasks and calendar items, are sent over an encrypted connection to the Study Tracker server, which forwards them to an AI service (the DeepSeek model, accessed through OpenRouter) to write a reply.
  • This is used only to answer you. It is not used for advertising and not sold.
  • Our server keeps only a daily count of how many AI messages each account sent (to prevent abuse and control cost) — not the content. Your chat history is stored on your device only.
  • The AI never changes your data by itself: every suggested change needs your “Apply” tap.
  • Please don’t enter private medical or patient information into the AI.

Technical logs

Like any website, our server records standard technical logs (such as IP address, time and the page requested) to keep the service running and secure. These are kept for a short time and are not used to identify or profile you.

Other permissions

The app may ask to send notifications (to tell you when a focus session ends). It does not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone, and it contains no advertising or tracking code.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account data until you delete it. You can delete your account and all of its synced data at any time inside the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. This removes your data from Firebase immediately. More details: how to delete your account.

Security

All connections use HTTPS. Your synced data is protected by database rules that allow access only to your own signed-in account.

Children

Study Tracker is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
